# Colloquial and Formal Word Pairs

Every pair lists the colloquial form first and the formal form second.
Most pairs come from the shekar informal words list, with additions from
the MahtaFetrat informal text detector word list and from common usage.
Pairs appear once, in the group where they fit best.

## Vowel shift words

Most of these follow the ā to u shift: a long a becomes u, often near n.
This is a pattern in frequent words, not a rule to apply everywhere.
Source: shekar `informal_words.csv`, deduplicated to 195 unique pairs.
