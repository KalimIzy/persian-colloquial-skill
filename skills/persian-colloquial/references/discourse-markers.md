# Discourse Markers

Discourse markers organize attitude and conversation. They show how the
speaker feels about what they are saying, or how one turn relates to the
next. They cannot be translated literally, and a sentence with the wrong
marker sounds off even when every other word is right. Use at most one
marker per short sentence.

## خب (khob)

Function: starts or resumes a topic, or moves the conversation forward.
It is the most frequent discourse marker in Persian speech.

- Formal: می‌خواهم موضوع را شروع کنم.
- Colloquial: خب، بذار شروع کنیم.

## دیگه (dige)

Function: closes a topic, marks something as settled, or adds a sense of
"already" or "anyway". Often softens a request or a refusal.

- Formal: دیگر نمی‌خواهم در این باره صحبت کنم.
- Colloquial: دیگه نمی‌خوام درباره‌ش حرف بزنم.

## آخه (ākhe)

Function: explains a reason the listener should already see, often with
a touch of complaint or surprise. It introduces a justification.

- Formal: نمی‌توانم بیایم، زیرا کار دارم.
- Colloquial: آخه کار دارم، نمی‌تونم بیام.

## که (ke)

Function: links clauses like "that", and in speech it also marks a
sentence as obvious or already known. At the end of a sentence it can
soften a statement into shared knowledge.

- Formal: می‌دانم که می‌آیی.
- Colloquial: می‌دونم که میای دیگه.

## بابا (bābā)

Function: a vocative that calls for attention or expresses exasperation.
It does not mean father here. It softens a complaint between friends.

- Formal: لطفا به حرف من گوش بده.
- Colloquial: بابا، گوش بده دیگه.

## والا (vāllā)

Function: "by God", used to insist that something is true, or to shrug
and say "I really do not know". Very frequent in speech.

- Formal: واقعا نمی‌دانم.
- Colloquial: والا نمی‌دونم.

## راستی (rāsti)

Function: "by the way", introduces a side topic or something the speaker
just remembered. It changes the subject without a hard break.

- Formal: به هر حال، باید بگویم که فردا نمی‌آیم.
- Colloquial: راستی، فردا نمیام.

## یعنی (ya'ni)

Function: "I mean", clarifies or restates what the speaker just said.
In fast speech it shortens to ینی.

- Formal: منظورم این است که دیر می‌رسم.
- Colloquial: یعنی دیر می‌رسم، ینی حدود ساعت هشت.

## مگه (mage)

Function: asks a surprised question, often expecting the answer no.
It challenges an assumption the listener seems to hold.

- Formal: آیا واقعا نمی‌دانستی؟
- Colloquial: مگه نمی‌دونستی؟

## اصلا (aslan)

Function: "at all", strengthens a negative, or dismisses something
entirely. In fast speech it shortens to اصن.

- Formal: اصلا مهم نیست.
- Colloquial: اصن مهم نیست.

## ببین (bebin)

Function: "look", calls for attention before an important point. It
opens an explanation or a correction.

- Formal: به این نکته توجه کن: فردا جلسه داریم.
- Colloquial: ببین، فردا جلسه داریم.

## هی (hey)

Function: "hey", calls someone or expresses mild surprise. Short and
informal, mostly used between friends.

- Formal: سلام، آیا می‌توانی کمکم کنی؟
- Colloquial: هی، می‌تونی کمکم کنی؟

## وای (vāy)

Function: "wow" or "oh", expresses surprise, delight, or dismay.
The meaning comes from tone and context.

- Formal: چقدر شگفت‌انگیز است!
- Colloquial: وای، چه باحال!
