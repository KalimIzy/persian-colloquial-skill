# Verb Tables: Top 40 High-Frequency Verbs

Full conjugation tables for the 40 verbs you will use most in
everyday Tehran colloquial Persian. Forms were generated with the
shekar Conjugator and checked by hand against known correct
colloquial forms. For the complete list of all verbs, see
[verb-tables-full.md](verb-tables-full.md).

Reading the tables:

- The 3rd person plural shown uses the ن ending (می‌رن). The ین
  variant (می‌رین) is also heard.
- Spelling uses the ZWNJ (می‌رم). Chat spelling often drops it (میرم).
- The imperative row shows singular, plural, and the colloquial
  plural variant with ین.
