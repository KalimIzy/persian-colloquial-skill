# Complete Verb Tables: All Verbs

Every verb from shekar `verbs.csv`, with its four stems and its
colloquial present tense for all six persons. Forms were generated
with the shekar Conjugator (version 1.8.0) from the stems in
`data/verbs.csv`. The top 40 verbs in [verb-tables.md](verb-tables.md)
were checked by hand against known correct Tehran colloquial forms.

How to read the table:

- A dash means the colloquial stem is the same as the formal stem.
- The 3rd person plural shown uses the ن ending (میرن). The ین
  variant (میرین) is also heard.
- Colloquial spelling is attached (میرم), the way people type. The
  ZWNJ form (می‌رم) is the careful variant for edited text.
- Rare and prefixed verbs were generated mechanically. The top 40
  verbs in [verb-tables.md](verb-tables.md) were checked by hand.

Total verbs: 341 unique infinitives, from 352 stem pairs in the source data. Eleven verbs
have two stem-pair entries in the source (for example گفتن with
present stems گو and گوی); the table lists each once, using the
stem shown in the table. The full source data, including both
entries, is in [data/verbs.csv](../data/verbs.csv).
